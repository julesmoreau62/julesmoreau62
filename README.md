## 👾 About me

I'm an **M2 International Sport Administration student** at Université de Lille (STAPS), 2025–2027.

I started in club communication, partnerships and events. On every field project I ended up building the tool the team was missing: a Notion handover, a template system, a partner website. Now that's the job I want: **mapping how sport organisations really work, automating what slows them down, and helping teams adopt AI tools.**

My M2 thesis studies **generative AI and no-code tools as levers of professionalisation for sports associations with limited resources**.

## 🛠️ What I build

### 🧠 [Thesis Engine](https://www.julesmoreau.eu/contracts/thesis-engine) · *live, private repo*
Local AI research pipeline behind my M2 thesis. Three scans a day across **Google Scholar, OpenAlex and Cairn** (~90 sources/day), triaged by a **LoRA fine-tuned Qwen3 4B** served 24/7 with Ollama + RAG, escalating complex analyses to **Claude Sonnet**. Everything lands in an Obsidian vault: notes, mind maps, bibliography. Cost ≈ €0/month.

### 🏑 [Royal Daring HC](https://www.julesmoreau.eu/contracts/daring) · *Brussels, 2026*
A sponsor system built to outlive my internship: bilingual FR/NL partner website, brand guidelines, Canva template system, 5-tier sponsorship offer and a **Notion handover** (playbook, editorial charter, 11-format content library).
